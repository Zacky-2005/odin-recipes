Kenyan Chicken Stew


Description

Kenyan chicken stew is a comforting dish made with chicken simmered in a sauce of tomatoes, onions, garlic, and spices. The sauce develops flavour as the chicken cooks until tender. The stew is often served with rice, chapati, ugali, or potatoes, making it a versatile meal for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients — approximately 4 servings

  • 700 g chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger (optional)
  • 3 ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder or paprika, to taste
  • 1 cup water or stock, adding more if needed
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Chopped coriander (optional)

Steps

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot and brown the chicken pieces on the outside.
  3. Add the chopped onion and cook until softened.
  4. Stir in the garlic, ginger if using, and curry powder or paprika.
  5. Add the tomatoes and tomato paste, then cook until the tomatoes soften into a thick sauce.
  6. Return or stir the chicken through the sauce, then add the water or stock.
  7. Cover and simmer until the chicken is fully cooked and tender, stirring occasionally and adding a little water if necessary.
  8. Taste and adjust the seasoning. Add chopped coriander if desired, then serve hot.
