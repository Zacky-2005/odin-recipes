Kenyan Beef Pilau.

Description

Kenyan beef pilau is a fragrant rice dish cooked with beef, onions, garlic, and warm spices. The rice absorbs the seasoned cooking liquid, giving the dish its characteristic brown colour and rich aroma. It is often served at family gatherings and celebrations. It can be enjoyed on its own or with a fresh tomato and onion salad.

Ingredients — approximately 4 servings

  • 2 cups of basmati or other long-grain rice
  • 500 g beef, cut into small pieces
  • 2 medium onions, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger
  • 2 medium potatoes, peeled and cubed (optional)
  • 1-2 teaspoons pilau masala, to taste
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Approximately 4 cups of water or stock, adjusted for the rice
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs relatively clear, then drain it.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot and brown the beef pieces.
  3. Add the onions and cook until softened and lightly browned.
  4. Stir in the garlic, ginger, pilau masala, cumin, and salt.
  5. Add the potatoes if using, then add enough water or stock to cook the beef until tender.
  6. Add the rice and stir gently so the spices are distributed.
  7. Add or adjust the liquid according to the rice used, bring to a boil, then cover and cook over low heat until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed.
  8. Turn off the heat and let the pilau rest, covered, for several minutes before fluffing it with a fork and serving.
