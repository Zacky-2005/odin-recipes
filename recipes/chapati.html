Kenyan Chapati.

Description

Kenyan chapati is a soft, layered flatbread made from wheat flour, water, salt, and oil. The dough is kneaded, rolled out, folded, and cooked on a hot pan until golden spots appear. Chapati is commonly served with stews, beans, lentils, or vegetables. It can be eaten at breakfast, lunch, or dinner.

Ingredients — approximately 6 chapatis

  • 3 cups all-purpose wheat flour, plus extra for rolling
  • Approximately 1 cup warm water, added gradually
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil for the dough
  • Additional oil for brushing and cooking

Steps

  1. Mix the flour and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add the oil and gradually add warm water, mixing until a dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough until it becomes smooth and elastic.
  4. Cover it and let it rest for about 20-30 minutes.
  5. Divide the dough into six portions and roll each portion into a thin circle.
  6. Brush lightly with oil, then fold or roll the dough to create layers. Coil it if desired, and roll it out again.
  7. Heat a pan over medium heat and cook each chapati, turning it and brushing lightly with oil as needed.
  8. Continue cooking until both sides have golden-brown spots and the chapati is cooked through.
